## Prompt

In the Oslo Home Assistant installation, give me a car charging summary for the last 30 days.
The car charger is on a metered electric plug.

Include:
- total charging energy over the period
- number of charging sessions
- a compact session table with date, start time, end time, duration, peak power and energy.

Do not ask me for entity IDs. Resolve the correct device and entities yourself and keep the answer concise.

## Evaluation Criteria

### Success Criteria

- Resolves the outdoor EV charger correctly even if the device is named differently, such as a charging plug.
- Uses the correct power and energy entities without asking the user for entity IDs.
- Returns total charging energy for the last 30 days.
- Returns the number of charging sessions (something like above 100 W and at least 5 minutes long).
- Returns a compact session table with date, start time, end time, duration, peak power, and energy.
- Keeps the answer concise and avoids exploratory noise.

### Expected Tool Path

- Preferred discovery path: one `ha_history_list_entities` or `ha_history_list_devices` call scoped to the outdoor charger concept.
- If `ha_history_list_devices` is used first, at most one follow-up discovery call should be needed to confirm the exact entities.
- One `ha_history_get_consumption` call for total energy.
- One `ha_history_detect_sessions` call for session count and compact session details.
- Avoids unrelated tools and avoids repeated device-by-device exploration.

### Follow-up Handling

- For follow-ups, reuses the resolved charger entities instead of rediscovering them.
- If the user asks for narrower thresholds, different date ranges, or different session columns, the assistant should answer with the minimal additional analytics calls needed.
